# Armory design council: pitch round 1

You are one of three independent design judges for ClickAway, a browser click-precision arena game. Its Armory page is where the player builds a machine (core / lens / rig parts, mods, three powerup keys) and test-fires it. You review the **Armory page only**, using screenshots. Do not edit any files and do not read source code.

1. View the four quality references in `docs/ui-polish/standards/`.
2. View the four CURRENT Armory screenshots, 1440x900, in `docs/ui-polish/council2-p1/`:
   - `armory.png`: the default Armory view
   - `mods.png`: the Mods step with a part featured
   - `powers.png`: the Powerups step
   - `testrun.png`: the Test Run step

## Your job
First, score the CURRENT Armory screen out of 10. 10 is best-in-class shipped game UI; 9 fits comfortably beside the references; 8 is a polished indie game with a few rough edges; 7 is competent but partly reads as a web app or template; 6 is a well-styled dark dashboard; 5 or below is generic or unfinished. Do not inflate.

Then **pitch three changes**. You are competing with two other judges. The implementer reads all nine pitches and builds only the three most compelling, so you must convince them yours are worth building. A strong pitch:
- names a specific region and says exactly what it becomes (what to move, merge, resize, restyle, redraw, add or remove);
- explains **how** it would be built well enough that the implementer can picture the result;
- argues **why** it is a significant improvement: what problem a player sees today, and what the screen gains (hierarchy, focus, use of space, readability, game-workshop feel);
- is visible in a still screenshot. Layout, composition, typography, colour, icons, illustration and surface treatment are all fair game. Animation and motion are not, because they cannot be judged from screenshots.

Small tweaks lose to big wins. Pitches that only restate a general principle lose to pitches with a concrete picture.

Return at most about 600 words, in exactly this shape:

OVERALL: <one decimal>/10
VERDICT: <2-3 sentences>
PITCH 1: <title>
- What: ...
- How: ...
- Why: ...
PITCH 2: <title>
- What / How / Why as above
PITCH 3: <title>
- What / How / Why as above
