# Armory layout council review (r58)

You are an independent design reviewer for ClickAway, a browser click-precision arena game. Its Armory page is where the player builds a machine (core / lens / rig parts, mods, three powerup keys) and test-fires it. Review the **layout and design of the Armory page only**, using screenshots. Do not edit any files and do not read source code.

1. View the four quality references in `docs/ui-polish/standards/`.
2. View the four current Armory screenshots, 1440x900, in `docs/ui-polish/r58/`:
   - `armory-before.png`: the default Armory view
   - `mods-before.png`: the Mods step with a part featured
   - `powers-before.png`: the Powerups step
   - `testrun-before.png`: the Test Run step

Score the Armory layout out of 10. 10 is best-in-class shipped game UI; 9 fits comfortably beside the references; 8 is a polished indie game with a few rough edges; 7 is competent but partly reads as a web app or template; 6 is a well-styled dark dashboard; 5 or below is generic or unfinished. Do not inflate.

Judge the layout itself: hierarchy and focal point, how the space is used (dead zones, cramped areas, competing panels), how the parts of the page relate, readability, and whether it feels like a game workshop rather than a settings form.

Return at most about 350 words, in exactly this shape:

OVERALL: <one decimal>/10
VERDICT: <2-3 sentences>
TOP CHANGE: <the single most valuable significant layout/design change: what to move, merge, resize, or restructure, and why. Be concrete (name the regions/panels as they appear).>
OTHER CHANGES: <3-5 further specific improvements, one line each>
