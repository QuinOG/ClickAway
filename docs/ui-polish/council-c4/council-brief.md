## What changed since last cycle (these came from the previous council's recommendations)
- r64: the part cards became labelled sockets on the ring. The three mod cards under the ring (which repeated each mount's emblem) are now name plates docked beside their mounts and still click to edit, and the machine is ~15% larger.
- r65: the ring's centre is a live aim-window readout. The target is drawn at the build's start size, and its centre states that size, the shrink pace and the floor.
- r66: the inspector is one surface that fills its column. The nested lane, featured-card and swap-impact frames flattened into the step surface (featured part separated by a divider), the panel runs to the bottom of the column, and Test Run centres its launch group.
