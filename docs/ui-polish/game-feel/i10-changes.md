# Round 10 changes (i9 → i10)

Picks came from `i10-decisions.md`. Every claim below was checked in a 100% crop of the 1440px capture.

## Regressions fixed
- **Login (01):** the red S1 stamp sits fully inside the pass, below the stub.
- **Shop (04):** the price appears once per item, on the paper tag. The buttons now read only "Unlock", "Equip" or "Equipped".
- **Profile (05):** locked trophies show only a count (for example "32 / 50"), with no bar.

## A. Settings (10)
- **Toggles:** they are rocker switches, 78px wide. A steel or gold ridged paddle tilts to one side, and the other side shows a lit lamp: green "ON" or red "OFF".
- **Toggle rows:** each row sits in a recessed dark well.
- **Volume:** the volume sliders are faders. Each has a black slot, a printed tick scale under the slot, and a ridged steel cap 38px tall. The percentage is gold, in the display face.
- **Intensity:** Reduced, Standard and High are three key caps in a recessed tray. The selected key is pressed in, gold and glowing.
- **Test cue:** the button is a full-width speaker grille, a dot-perforated panel 84px tall with a "TEST CUE" label plate. It fills the space that used to be empty under the Audio panel.
- **Reset defaults:** this is a red maintenance key.

## B. Profile (05)
- **Gauges:** the four attribute rows are now four side-by-side gauge plates. Each plate holds a 180° dial with a cyan arc, tick marks and a red needle, then a 30px value, then the label. The signature attribute (Reaction) has a gold arc on a gold plate.
- **Height:** the attribute block is about 170px tall, down from about 330px.
- **Scouting report:** Recent form, Top build and Nearest achievement share one recessed housing with a gold "SCOUTING REPORT" swallowtail tab. Dashed dividers separate the three.

## C. Shop (04)
- **Box body:** each box's body is printed in its rarity colour (steel grey, blue, violet, or bronze-gold) instead of the shared dark navy.
- **Side face:** each box has a solid 9px side face to the lower right in a darker shade, outlined in ink, so it reads as a box with depth.
- **Frame:** the frame band has visible brushed streaks.
- **Epic flecks:** epic frames carry white star flecks.
- **Legendary glow:** legendary boxes glow amber, with a 36px spread.
- **Window glare:** two glare streaks cross each item window, at 34% and 20% opacity.

## D. Duels (07)
- **Ticket centre:** the empty middle of the fight ticket is printed with "GHOST DUEL · ADMIT TWO", a serial ("No. 000041") and "CASUAL RULES". The block has a dashed border, is rotated −4°, and is set in faded cream ink.
- **Counter:** the "Stake a run" row sits on a wooden counter with planks and a light top edge, under a gold "STAKE A RUN" label plate. The taunt and Send row sits on a dark wooden ledge below it.

## E. History (08)
- The two Highlights cards are brass plaques: a gold-brass gradient with a fine brushed grain, a dark inner rim and a light bevel. The text is engraved in dark brown. The stat chips are recessed into the brass.

## F. Help (09)
- **Contents:** the "Field guide" index is a cream paper page titled "Contents · Field guide", with the six coloured chapter ribbons on it.
- **Plates:** every dark illustration plate on the chapter pages is mounted with a cream border, an ink outline and an offset shadow on the paper.
