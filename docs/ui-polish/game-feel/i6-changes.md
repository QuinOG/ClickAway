# Round 6 changes (i5 → i6)

Picks came from `i6-decisions.md`. Council is now Sol plus Opus 5 (low effort). Descriptions are literal.

## Regressions
- **Ladder (06):** the plates are shorter (64px, was 78; podium plates 70–76px), with a 10px gap (was 14).
- **Duels (07):** the fight card is shorter: 22px padding (was 34), 124px portraits (was 150), an 80px VS (was 96), and 112px run tokens (was 128).
- **History (08):** the dashed tear line at the stub seam is now 75% white (was 45%).
- **Arena warm glow (11):** the bottom heat glow now has a fixed amber layer and a minimum opacity of 55% (was 35%).

## A. Arena materials (11 live round, 02 Play lobby)
- The court is now hardwood: 96px planks in three browns (#7a4c26, #6a4020, #73471f) with dark seams, grain lines inside each plank, and an end joint every 311px. The gold centre circle, centre line and baseline sit on the wood, with an amber pool under the centre.
- The light cones from the two floodlight banks are blurred 10px, so they read as diffuse light, not hard triangles.
- The Play lobby now uses the same wood court behind the target preview, at 30° perspective. The outer dashed orbit ring is removed, and the inner ring is now a solid gold line. In i5 this floor was claimed but never painted; it now is.

## E. Profile (05)
The cockpit header and the KPI tiles are replaced by a character sheet.
- **Left: a competitor card.**
  - A gold "COMPETITOR CARD · LV 12" banner.
  - A 200px portrait slab with the division crest (96px, gold ring) overlapping its corner.
  - A 46px name, the title ribbon, and a recessed division plate (Gold II, 64/100 RR).
  - Next rank, the XP bar, and the coin/milestone/log-out buttons.
- **Right: "Attributes".** Four bolted metal rows (Accuracy, Best streak, Reaction, Peak score), each with:
  - an icon medallion
  - a 12-pip gauge
  - a 34px value
  The signature strength row (Reaction) is a gold plate with gold pips and a "SIGNATURE" tag.
- The Recent form bar chart is gone. The last 7 rounds are now 52px coin tokens with the score on each: gold for ranked, blue for casual, red for an RR loss.
- Top build and Nearest achievement are two compact plates with medallions under the form row.
- Achievement medals now have a different silhouette per tier: bronze round, silver shield, gold star, platinum hexagon, diamond lozenge, master crown.

## D. Shop arena-theme previews (04)
- Each Arena Theme card's preview now shows a miniature of the actual stadium: truss, scoreboard, stands, crowd and floodlights on a wood court with the gold centre circle. Each theme re-tints it: Sunset warm red, Forest green, Arcade violet. Before, it was an empty wireframe floor grid.

## B2. Help chapters 4 and 5 (09)
- CH.4 Progression and Economy opens with three drawn cards: Earn XP (gem and XP bar), Spend coins (coin stack to gift box), and Climb ranks (shield and RR arrow).
- CH.5 Account opens with three: Profile (a mini competitor card), History (a ticket stub), and Ladder (a stack of plates with yours lit).
- Before, both chapters showed only a collapsed "Full rulebook" drawer.
