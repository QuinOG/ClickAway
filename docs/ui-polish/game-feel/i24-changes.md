# Round 24 changes (i23 → i24)

Every claim below was checked in a 100% crop and passed a blind before/after check (help +0.6, settings +0.5, results +0.7). Screens 09, 10 and 12 changed; every other file in i24 is a byte-for-byte copy of i23.

## A. Help (09)
- **Field-guide spreads:** each chapter (First round, Core gameplay, Modes and ranked, Progression and economy, Account and navigation) is a ruled parchment page with a ring-bound spine, a CH.N tab and a large outlined chapter numeral.
- Steps are numbered (01, 02, 03…) with an inline illustration above each: mode tickets, countdown, target and cursor, rewards; hit, chain multiplier bars, miss and STREAK 0; XP bar, coins into gift, rank shield with RR arrow.
- Power keys are numbered entries on the same paper; FAQ questions are numbered paper slips.

## B. Settings (10)
- **Volume faders:** Master and Effect volume no longer sit in their own boxed cards. Each is a rail directly on the panel with an engraved scale (major and minor ticks), split from its neighbours by engraved dividers like the toggles.
- Labels are engraved uppercase; values sit in small green-lit readouts; the fader caps are gold.

## C. Results (12)
- **One clean-run citation:** the corner CLEAN badge and the CLEAN RUN pill under the score are gone. CLEAN RUN appears once, as a large title on a tilted cream citation card with a red CITATION tab, beside a larger glowing medal, with the hit chain and "16 HITS / 0 MISSES" on the card.
