# Round 5 changes (i4 → i5)

Picks came from `i5-decisions.md` (weighted table). Descriptions are literal; compare against the pixels.

## Regressions fixed first
- **Bubble skin (04 Shop):** the catalog drew it at 250% scale, which cropped its outline and rim, so only the pale centre showed in i4. It now uses 120% like the other items. The SVG was also redrawn: clear centre, a 10px rim with a cyan → pink → yellow → mint gradient, a 4.5px ink line and two white glints.
- **History ticket notch (08):** the punched half-circles at the stub seam are now 12px with a 2px light rim, and the tear line is a 3px dashed line at 45% white (was 80% dark ink, invisible).
- **Arena warm floor glow (11):** an amber pool is painted onto the floor under the centre circle. The streak heat glow now stays at a minimum of 35% opacity instead of dropping to zero.

## A. Arena (11 live round, also behind the 02 Play lobby)
- The cyan wireframe is gone: the canopy lines, X-braced side rigs, horizon pylons and target rails are removed.
- Roof: two filled steel-gradient chords with a dark steel truss web between them. Two steel columns run down to the side stands.
- Two floodlight banks of 4 warm lamps hang under the truss, each throwing a soft light cone down onto the floor.
- Scoreboard: hung on two cables, 252×82 housing with speaker boxes, a lit blue screen with scanlines, and 12 blinking amber bulbs under it. It reads LIVE when idle. Streak callouts (HOT / ON FIRE / UNSTOPPABLE) now show as solid glowing text on that screen; before, they were a faint outline.
- Stands are filled tiered blocks with seat-row stripes and three aisles. The crowd silhouettes are unchanged.
- An LED barrier board runs in front of the stands with an amber strip and the text "CLICKAWAY ★ PRECISION ARENA ★ SEASON 1".
- Banners now hang on poles and carry a round emblem.
- The floor grid is replaced by a painted court: 96px alternating boards with dark seams, a gold centre line, a gold centre circle, and a baseline. The perspective is flattened, so the court fills the lower half instead of a thin strip.

## B. Help (09)
- Quick Start (4 text cards) and Round Flow (6 numbered cards) are replaced by one 4-panel illustrated strip: Pick a mode → Count in → Hit and chain → Cash in.
  - Each panel has its own drawing (mode cards, 3-2-1 dial, target trail with ×4, medal and coins), a gold numbered medallion, and gold arrows between panels.
  - The old text cards moved into that section's Full rulebook drawer.
- Each section now opens with a gold "CH.1 … CH.6" plate, a 26px display-font title and a gold rule.
- The six Field guide tiles are now coloured bookmark ribbons with notched bottoms.

## F. Duels (07)
- The composer is no longer a three-step form (01 / 02 / 03 rows are gone). It is now a fight card:
  - The challenger's portrait (150px, blue ring) on a blue diagonal half, and the rival's (red ring, "?" until a name is typed) on a red half.
  - A 96px VS between them, with the "score to beat" plate under it.
  - The rival username field sits inside the rival's red nameplate.
- Runs are three 128px round tokens in mode colours (Ranked gold, Casual blue, Practice green). The selected token lifts and gets a gold ring and check.
- The message field is a rounded "taunt" bar next to the Send Duel button.

## C. Ladder (06)
- The sortable table and its column-header row are gone. Each player is a bolted metal plate with:
  - a shield-shaped rank crest
  - a 44px portrait
  - name and tier badge
  - four recessed stat chips
  - a large stat window on the right showing the active tab's stat
- Ranks 1, 2 and 3 have gold, silver and bronze plates. Your own plate is blue, wider than the others, ringed in glowing cyan, and tagged "YOU ARE HERE".
- Sort direction is a single "High to low ▼" toggle under the tabs.

## Untouched (PROTECT)
Armory rig (03), Results (12), the History ticket layout, the Shop item family (bubble aside), and the Login logo lockup.
