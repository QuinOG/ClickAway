# Round 12 changes (i11 → i12)

Picks came from `i12-decisions.md`. Every claim below was checked in a 100% crop of the 1440px capture.

## A. Profile (05)
- **Scouting report height:** the report no longer stretches to fill the column. It sits at its natural height, and the right column now ends level with the competitor card.
- **Recent form:** the form is a column chart in a recessed dark tray with grid lines and a blue baseline.
  - Each round's score coin stands on a coloured column: gold for ranked, blue for casual, red for an RR loss.
  - Column height scales from the worst to the best run in the window, so 638 is short and 920 is tallest.
- **Trend:** the +24% figure sits in a black LED box at the right of the "Recent form · Last 7 rounds" header.
- **Sub-plaques:** Top Build and Nearest Achievement are separate framed plaques. Each has a gold inner trim, four corner screws and a dark outline.
- **Trophy shelf:** the wooden shelf runs directly under the trophies, which now stand on it. A dark contact shadow sits on the shelf under each trophy. The ribbons hang in front of the shelf edge, and the titles sit below it.

## B. Duels (07)
- **Planks:** the counter is now drawn as wooden planks in place of the turbulence texture. The planks are laid in two staggered rows with dark seams, butt joints, light top edges, a few grain lines and knots.
- **Lamp:** a lamp pool lights the middle of the counter, and the edges fall off to dark.
- **Token shadows:** the three run tokens cast hard shadows away from the lamp. The left token's shadow falls down and to the left, the middle one straight down, and the right one down and to the right.
- **Lower ledge:** the ledge behind the taunt and Send row uses the same planks, stained dark.
- **Front lip:** the counter's front lip is a clean bevel with no texture.

## C. Ladder (06)
- **Stats:** the four stats on each row sit in one recess engraved into the row plate. The recess has an inset shadow, a lit lower edge in the plate's trim colour and engraved dividers. The labels take the trim colour.
- **Rating:** the rating window is an engraved recess with a lit lower lip, with no separate bordered box.
- **Nameplates:** nameplates have no brushed grain. Rows outside the podium (for example, Prism) get a light steel nameplate, so the dark name text is legible.

## D. History (08)
- **Bezel:** the chart spans the full width. The "Ranked trajectory +282" placard sits inside the chart bezel's header strip at top left, and the Practice / Casual / Ranked switches sit in a recessed track at top right. A rule separates the header strip from the plot.
- **Axis:** a score axis runs on the left with three labels (920, 725 and 530 for Ranked). Each label has a dashed guide line across the plot.
- **Labels:** the "SCORE TREND" and "LIVE" labels were removed. The crown above the best score sits clear of the switches.
- **Plaques:** the two Highlights plaques are smooth brass with no brushed grain behind the small engraved text.

## E. Play (02)
- **Mode cards:** each mode card is a plaque with four corner screws and two diagonal stripes at the right in the mode's colour. The mode icon sits in a round medallion with a ring in the mode colour.
- **Record:** the Casual record is an LED scoreboard. It has a dot-matrix black face, a double bezel and an amber "CASUAL RECORD" title. Best, Average and Rounds are amber LED numbers in black wells. The bars are stacked amber LED segments, with the latest run in pale yellow.
