# Round 7 changes (i6 → i7)

Picks came from `i7-decisions.md`. Council: Sol (med), Opus 5 (low), Gemini 4 Argon (med), Qwen3.8 Max (med). Descriptions are literal.

## C. Ladder (06)
- **Podium:** the three leader cards are replaced by a stepped podium. Each player stands on a solid block: gold for 1st (132px tall), silver for 2nd (96px), bronze for 3rd (72px). Each block has a lit top lip and a large outlined "1st", "2nd" or "3rd". A warm, blurred spotlight cone falls on 1st place. The faint target rings behind the podium are removed.
- **Your standing:** it is now one blue rank plate with corner rivets. A cyan shield shows "SPOT #4". The six stats sit in one recessed bar with divider lines and gold numerals, where before they were six separate tiles.
- **Search:** a framed field (see G) with a magnifier cap.

## B1. Global nav (all signed-in screens)
- The top bar has a gold trim line along its bottom edge.
- The five main tabs sit in a recessed tray. Each tab is a steel plaque with two rivets. The active tab is a gold plaque with dark text; the cyan underline is gone.
- Profile, History, Help and Settings are now round steel medallions with gold rings. Each has its name visible underneath; before, they were unlabeled glyphs. The active medallion turns gold.
- The player plate has a gold inner rim, four corner rivets, a gold-ringed portrait and a gold rank label. Coins and level sit in recessed slots.

## A. Shop storefront (04)
- **Inspection stage:** the selected item now stands in a 320px display stage that takes more than half the hero width.
  - Behind it, a dim, theme-tinted miniature of the stadium.
  - A spotlight cone falls on the item, which is shown at 176px (was 102px).
  - It sits on a turntable pedestal with a gold rim and visible thickness.
  - When a profile image is selected, the portrait takes the pedestal instead. When an arena theme is selected, the stadium behind it is shown at full strength.
- **Item text:** the item name is now 34px in the display face.
- **Category tabs:** they are physical folder tabs standing on a wooden plank. The active tab is a raised gold tab. Item counts are gold coins.
- **Category shelves:** each category is a recessed display cabinet with its title on a wooden sign with nails. The item cards and their art are unchanged (protected).

## D. Armory right rail (03)
- **Stat strip:** each of the five stats is a recessed, riveted hardware slot. Each has a 10-segment gauge under the number. During a swap preview, the segments being gained light mint.
- **Swap report:** each row has a 10-segment gauge under its label. Gains light mint and losses show red-striped segments. The exact numbers (current and new) are unchanged (protected).

## G. Framed inputs (01 login, 06 ladder search, 07 duels taunt)
- Text inputs are recessed dark slots with an ink border and a thin gold rim. Each has a steel icon cap on the left: user and lock on Login, a magnifier on Ladder, a speech bubble on the Duels taunt. A focused field gets a gold ring.
