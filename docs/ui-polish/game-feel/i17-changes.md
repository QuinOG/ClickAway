# Round 17 changes (i16 → i17)

Every claim below was checked in a 100% crop and passed a blind before/after check. Only screens 03, 08 and 09 changed; every other file in i17 is a byte-for-byte copy of i16.

## A. Help (09)
- **FAQ:** the CH.6 FAQ is a two-column grid of paper index cards instead of one lined sheet of paragraphs. Each card has a red margin line, a strip of tape at the top, a stamped red "Q1"–"Q9" badge, the question in display type and the answer below.
- **Rulebook:** every "Full rulebook" toggle is a brass plate with screw heads at both ends instead of a flat dark bar.

## B. History (08)
- **Load more:** "Load older rounds" is a wide beveled brass plate, centred under the tickets.
- **Averages:** the "Last 8 rounds" strip is a recessed LED panel; its values (Avg Score, Avg Accuracy, Net RR) glow in gold display digits.
- The trajectory chart and the ticket rail are unchanged.

## C. Armory (03)
- **Bezels:** the five stat cells (Aim window, Shrink pace, Combo ramp, Miss cost, Power tempo) and the SWAP REPORT panel have riveted steel frames (a rivet in each corner, a beveled top edge and a dark lower lip) instead of flat 1px borders. Their content and layout are unchanged.

## Correction after judging
- **Armory stat cells:** the riveted frames did not reach the five stat cells at the top of the right rail (their selector missed); only the SWAP REPORT panel got the frame. Sol caught it.
