# Round 36 changes (i35 → i36): spacing round 1

Spacing rounds are judged only on layout: placement, gaps, alignment, grouping and redundancy. The blind check passed for shop (+0.6), profile (+0.5), ladder (+0.7) and the ladder hover card (+0.4). Screens 04, 05, 06 and 15 changed; every other file in i36 is a byte-for-byte copy of i35.

## A. Profile (05)
- **One achievement heading:** the "Collection" label jammed against the "Achievement constellation" banner is gone; the banner and category tabs share one row with a clear 24px gap.
- **One signature marker:** the floating "Signature strength · Tempo" label is gone; the "Signature" tag on the Reaction gauge already says it.
- **Top build on one line:** "24 rounds · 93% acc · 1,040 peak" no longer wraps into three short lines.

## B. Ladder (06, 15)
- **Two control rows:** the five metric tabs fill one even row; search, Top Players / Around Me and the sort order share the second row.
- **One sort cue:** the orphaned "Sorted by rating" caption row is gone; the active tab and the "High to low" control already show it.
- **Standing card without repeats:** "Visible at #4" and "You are in the current results window" are gone (the #4 shield says it). The card now matches the podium height with its content centred.

## C. Shop (04)
- **Shorter Live loadout hero:** the preview is about 220px tall, so the category tabs and first product row sit higher.
- **No repeated counts:** the hero "Collection 4/27" block and the numbered circles on the tabs are gone; the tabs keep their "owned" fractions.
- **One equipped tag per card:** the duplicate "Equipped" card tag is gone; the button still says it.

## Not shipped
