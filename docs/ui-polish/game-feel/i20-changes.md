# Round 20 changes (i19 → i20)

Every claim below was checked in a 100% crop and passed a blind before/after check. Screens 03, 14, 15, 16 and 20 changed; every other file in i20 is a byte-for-byte copy of i19.

## A. Armory (03)
- **Telemetry:** the five stat cells (Aim window, Shrink pace, Combo ramp, Miss cost, Power tempo) are larger instrument faces in their riveted frames: engraved centred label, a recessed display window with 20px glowing digits, and a segment gauge underneath. On a previewed cell the current value sits small and dim with an arrow, and the new value is large in green or red; the green underline stays.
- The strip is about 30px taller, so the parts picker below starts lower.

## B. Competitor pass (14)
- The pass is one continuous card with a guilloche background: hero, rank and stats are no longer separate boxes but are split by engraved lines.
- The rank is named once (the duplicate "Gold II" under the name is gone); rank text is larger gold with a glowing RR track.
- Coins, Level and Accuracy are centred engraved readouts with engraved dividers.

## C. Ladder hover card (15)
- **Scouting dossier:** a tall framed portrait fills the left third; the name plate and rank sit beside it.
- Accuracy, best score and best streak are three riveted instruments with the value above the label: accuracy is a ring gauge filled to the percentage, best score is gold, best streak is orange.
- Coins, level and ranked rounds are a small footer row.

## D. Armory compare (16)
- **Paired instruments:** each of the seven stats shows two full-width bars with their own label and value: Active is lit cyan segments, Ghost is hatched violet segments in a dashed frame. Differing segments stay green (gain) or red (cost); the delta is a badge in the card's corner.
- **Report plates:** each loadout plate is about half as tall; Strengths and Tradeoffs each sit on one labelled line. The bench no longer scrolls.

## E. More menu (20)
- The subtitle under the ribbon is gone; Settings is a slim dark switch plate under the four destinations instead of a full-size tile.

## Not shipped
- **Spec sheet inspection form (18):** built (larger labelled assembly, ruled value rows, ink-outlined records), then reverted after the blind check judged the i19 dial cards better by 1.0. 18 is a byte-for-byte copy of i19.
