# Round 14 changes (i13 → i14)

Picks came from `i14-decisions.md`. Every claim below passed its ACCEPT line in a 100% crop and a blind before/after check. Only screens 14, 15, 16 and 20 changed; every other file in i14 is a byte-for-byte copy of i13.

## B. Ladder hover card (15)
- **Card:** a rival scouting card with a steel frame and corner screws. A teal (Platinum) header band carries "Nova" in the display face and a framed portrait medallion with her green visor helmet.
- **Rank:** a large Platinum crest beside "Platinum I", with "80 / 100 RR" over a segmented gauge.
- **Stats:** six engraved cells with fully written labels: Coins, Level, Accuracy, Best Score, Best Streak, Ranked Rounds. No label ends in "...".

## C. Identity popover (14)
- **Pass:** the dropdown is a miniature gold Competitor Pass with gold trim and a "COMPETITOR PASS" header strip.
- **Identity:** Vector's pilot portrait (about a quarter of the card width) over diagonal team stripes, with the name and Gold II.
- **Rank and stats:** a Gold II crest with "64 / 100 RR" over a segmented gold track; Coins, Level and the full word "Accuracy" with values; a beveled gold "Open full profile" button.

## D. Mobile More menu (20, 390px)
- **Plaques:** Shop, Duels, History, Help and Settings are beveled steel plaque tiles with corner screws in a two-column grid on a dark wood backing; Settings spans the last row.
- **Emblems:** each tile shows its full-colour illustrated icon (gift, swords, clock with scroll, help medallion, gear) in a recessed socket, with a category label and the destination name.
- **Header:** a gold ribbon "MORE DESTINATIONS" header with a red close medallion.

## E. Armory compare (16)
- **Rigs:** the empty ACTIVE / GHOST placeholder circles are replaced by two illustrated loadout rigs, each a gear hub with its three attached modules (Tempo Core above, Streak Lens left, Power Rig right) joined by leads and labelled with the part name. The Ghost rig's module frames and leads are dashed and dimmed, with readable labels.
- **Gauges:** the 7-row text table is replaced by seven framed instruments (Aim Window, Shrink Pace, Combo Ramp, Miss Cost, Power Tempo, Min Target, Score Mult). Each shows Active and Ghost values with segmented bars and the delta in the corner, coloured green or red.
- **Header and tabs:** "COMPARE BENCH · CASUAL" eyebrow over the title; "Bay vs. Bay" and "Mode Matrix" are beveled tabs, the active one in gold.
- Not claimed: arrows from each rig to every value (the leads join modules to the hub, not to the gauges).
