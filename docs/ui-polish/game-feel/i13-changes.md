# Round 13 changes (i12 → i13)

Picks came from `i13-decisions.md`. Every claim below passed its ACCEPT line in a 100% crop, and passed a blind before/after check. Screens 14–20 are new captures of existing small surfaces (identity popover, Ladder hover card, Armory compare / unlock wall / spec sheet, sign-up, mobile More menu) and have no previous shot.

## A. Shop (04)
- **Displays:** Each shop card displays its item resting on or above a visible mini pedestal/shelf surface rather than floating in a plain dark navy box. Button skins stand on round turntables with a colored glow around the rim. Arena themes are propped as postcards on a shelf lip. Profile images sit in portrait mounts on wooden bases.
- **Sections:** All three sections visibly use different interior display objects; prices, ownership labels and purchase buttons are unchanged and legible.
- **Tabs:** The 4 category tabs are beveled metal plaques with corner screws standing on the wooden plank (gold for the active tab).

## B. Results (12)
- **Medal and hit chain:** Beneath 37, a substantial clean-run medal and sixteen linked hit marks occupy the formerly empty burst area; the score remains the largest number.

## C. Live round (11)
- **Keycaps:** No ready banners protrude above the dock; keys 1–3 glow within their slots as keycaps.
- **Target halo:** The target has a crisp light outline ringed in dark, distinguishable from the floor lines at 100%.

## D. Play (02)
- **Target stand:** The lobby target is mounted on a drawn stand (post, base plate and cable) on the arena floor instead of floating.

## E. Competitor portraits (05, 06, 07, nav on every screen)
- **Archetypes:** Players are drawn from eight distinct illustrated portraits (visor helmet, cap and goggles, hood with scope, crested gladiator, headset pilot, …) instead of one repeated helmet. Each portrait has a shadow side, a rim light, eyes with catch-lights, brows and an expression, gear detail, and a team-striped shoulder pad. The signed-in player (Vector, on the Profile card) is a headset pilot with a pushed-up flight visor, grilled earcups and a foam-tipped boom mic. Ladder rows, the Duels challenger, the Profile card and the nav identity plate show them.

## F. Icons (every screen; most visible on 02, 11, 13)
- **Detail pass:** All shared icons gained a darker shadow side, metal bevels, rivets or facets and a crisp highlight, with silhouettes unchanged. This is clearest on the large emblems: the rank-up crest, the Play mode medallions and the power keycaps. At nav size the change is subtle.
