# Round 18 changes (i17 → i18)

Every claim below was checked in a 100% crop and passed a blind before/after check (Armory +0.4, History +0.8, Settings +0.6). Only screens 03, 08 and 10 changed; every other file in i18 is a byte-for-byte copy of i17.

## A. Armory (03)
- **Stat cells:** the five cells at the top of the right rail (Aim window, Shrink pace, Combo ramp, Miss cost, Power tempo) now have riveted steel frames (a rivet in each corner, a beveled top edge, a dark lower lip), matching the SWAP REPORT. This is the round 17 fix that missed. Previewed cells keep their green underline.

## B. History (08)
- **Ticket stubs:** each match ticket's right-hand stub, past the perforation, holds the result stamp (+14 RR, NO CHANGE, −8 RR) on a mode-tinted panel with punched notches, instead of the stamp sitting beside the score and the stub standing empty. "Latest" and "Peak score" markers sit under the stamp.
- **Chart:** the trajectory chart is about 50px shorter, so the log starts higher.

## C. Settings (10)
- **Control groups:** Sound cues and Arena feedback sit in corner-riveted metal bezels; the toggle rows lost their separate inset boxes and are split by engraved dividers.
- **Switches:** the ON/OFF block toggles are tilted rocker switches with an indicator light (green when on, red when off).
- **Footer:** Reset defaults and Close are beveled metal plates; the fader caps have more depth.
