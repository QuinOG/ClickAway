# Round 4 changes (g4 → g5)

1. **Results (loop-04):** the card is now two columns on desktop: score and rewards (XP, next unlock) on the left; hit split, personal best and all four performance stats on the right. Nothing scrolls under the action bar any more. The "Score Locked" badge is gone; the badge only appears for a PB or a clean run. The loadout line is smaller.
2. **Live play (loop-02/03):** the target now has a raised material look: a specular cap, a shaded lower lip, a drop shadow and a centre bullseye pip. Hits fire a short local shockwave and sparks at the click point. Hit numbers are smaller and fade faster, so they don't scatter across the arena.
3. **Armory rig (armory-06/07):** the empty dashed ring is replaced by a rig core around the target. A ring is split into three arcs coloured by slot. Each installed part's icon is mounted on its arc and glows when a non-stock part is fitted. Stock slots stay dim, so changing parts visibly changes the rig.
4. **Test run results (armory-08):** the duplicated caption is gone. There is now a "Test complete" headline with a score verdict ("+N score vs last run"). A tag reads "Repeat: same parts" or "A/B: parts changed". When parts change between runs, the columns read Old parts / New parts. "Keep build" is now "Adjust build", which returns to the builder.
5. **Unlock wall (armory-11):** a featured "Next unlock" banner shows the next part's icon, name, full benefit and how many levels away it is. The All tab is two columns, and descriptions wrap in full with no truncation. Locked parts are much less dim, show their benefit, and carry a small "Unlocks at Lv N" tag. The repeated "Collected" status chips are removed; only "Installed" is marked.
