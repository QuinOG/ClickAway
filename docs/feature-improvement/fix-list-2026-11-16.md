# Browser game test: fix list (user, 2026-11-16)

The user's notes, grouped by screen, kept as they wrote them.

## Armory

### Build Base and loadouts
- The **Build Base** area on the left looks low-quality, and they don't like the preview. It should feel more like Call of Duty classes.
- They like the cards the loadouts sit in, and having them numbered **1, 2 and 3** makes sense.
- The **Balance, Control and Pressure** labels may not be needed. A new player won't necessarily understand what they mean, so keep in mind what might confuse someone new.
- On Loadout 1 on a new account, the small loadout preview/button is hard to read. Things like **"Aim Window: 100 pixels"** and **"4% Hit Floor: 12 pixels"** confuse a new player, and the text itself is hard to read.
- The loadout stats look good overall.

### Tempo Core and Power Rig
- The **Tempo Core** looks okay. The **Power Rig** should use the same kind of card, or something similar, maybe with a yellow treatment.
- The Power Rig's card is cut off, and its words overflow on the right side.
- The yellow bars at the bottom of the Tempo Core don't really make sense.
- The yellow squares at the bottom of each Power-Up don't really make sense either.

### Other Armory screens
- The **Spec Sheet** seems fine.
- **Compare Modes** seems fine overall, but on that screen a lot of the text explaining which build suits which mode is dark and looks bad.
- In the **Mode Matrix**, Practice, Casual and Ranked all use the same lightning-bolt logo. Each should have its own.
- The logos sit outside the box. Move away from the boxed-in look: the aim is to get away from the SaaS-dashboard style with outlines around everything, so just show the logo.
- **Test Run** seems fine for now.

## Shop

### Navigation
- Sometimes the screen doesn't switch when moving between pages. It might only happen locally, but it's worth checking.

### Live Loadout preview
- The **Live Loadout** needs work. The shop stand is overbearing for something that's meant to be a preview.
- Remove the stand. Show just the button, the background and the profile.
- The profile picture could be a little bigger, and the background should come further forward.
- There's a blue bar in front of the button, but not in front of the arena or profile. Remove the blue bar altogether.

### Button-skin podiums
- They like the Legendary podium, and the purple one can stay.
- For the blue and grey ones: either swap them, or tone down the current blue one and make it grey, then use the grey one for blues.
- The podiums shouldn't be overbearing. Commons should be fairly simple and Uncommons a little nicer. Legendary can be more extravagant, and the purple one a little more extravagant too.

### Arena themes
- Drop the current styling for Arena Themes and make them match the Profile Images, which have a clipboard-like look the user likes.
- If ChatGPT can generate images for the cards and save a lot of code, do that. Otherwise the clipboard-style cards are fine.

## Profile
- The **View Milestones** button on the profile card is redundant (it only scrolls the page down). Remove it.
- The progress bars are underwhelming; look for a better solution. This covers the nearest achievement on the profile, the achievement bars, and the XP on the profile.
- Remove the small logos on the attributes. The logos in the top left are kind of pointless and add clutter; just show the percentage.
- A long username overflows the main profile card from left to right. Either shrink the letters or let the name break onto a new line, as it does in the Competitor Pass preview.

## History
- History looks pretty good.
- The highlight cards are hard to read, especially the smaller details. The user isn't sure about the dark text. They understand it's meant to look engraved, and the main text (**Peak Score, Latest Run, All-Rounder, 37 Score, +R**) looks fine.
- The supporting text is hard to read: **"8 hours ago", "100% accuracy", "22 hits", "32 coins"** and the milliseconds. Fix the readability of that text.

## Help page / Field Guide
- The **six systems** in the top right of the Help page seem kind of pointless.
- **"Updated for this season"** also seems kind of pointless.
- The **Contents / Field Guide** element seems kind of pointless too.
- Change **"Learn by Sight"** to **"Field Guide"**.
- The expansion still has very strong contrast, and the user doesn't like how it looks.
- The user isn't sure whether the Contents / Field Guide banners are hand-coded CSS or SVG, but those small banners could probably be images instead.

## Competitor Pass
- A long username in the Competitor Pass profile preview overlaps the small ClickAway target logo on the right.
- Possibly remove that logo and set a username limit. Usernames don't need to be longer than about **25–30 characters**.
- Long usernames also overflow the main profile card, so decide how to handle them: shrink the letters or let them wrap onto another line.
